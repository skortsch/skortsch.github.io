Foundation species such as bladderwrack (*Fucus vesiculosus*), seagrasses, and blue mussels create habitats that support diverse ecological communities.

Our research examines how these species regulate biodiversity, modify ecological interactions, and influence ecosystem processes such as productivity and carbon cycling.
