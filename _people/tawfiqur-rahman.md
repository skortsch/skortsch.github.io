About

I am a marine experimental ecologist and Postdoctoral Researcher at the 
University of Helsinki, based at Tvärminne Zoological Station. 
My research investigates how anthropogenic disturbances mainly ocean warming and species invasion alter species interactions, ecosystem structure, functioning and stability of coastal food webs. My work connects organismal responses to community dynamics, asking when climate stress and biological invasion amplify or reorganize food web effects.

My current research focuses on interactions between the native three-spined 
stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) and the invasive rockpool 
shrimp (Palaemon elegans) in the Baltic Sea. I combine mesocosm experiments,behavioural observations, and food-web approaches to examine how marine heatwaves influence competition, predation, habitat use, and ecosystem functioning.

## Ongoing project

### Marine heatwaves, species interactions and functional stability

I study if temperature mediated behavioural interaction facilitate Palaemon elegans invasion in the southern Finnish coast of Baltic ecosystem. 
Besides, using experimental mesocosms, I work on how marine temperature extremes influences the resistance, resilience, recovery, and temporal stability of different trophic groups.
