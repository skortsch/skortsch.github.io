## Research Interests

- Ecological networks
- Marine food webs
- Food web modeling
- Ecosystem functioning

## Biography

Susanne Kortsch is an Academy Research Fellow at the University of Helsinki based at Tvärminne Zoological Station. Her research combines field studies, experiments, ecological network analysis, and ecosystem modelling to understand how climate change alters ecosystems.

## Current Projects

- Heatwave-driven Rewiring of Coastal Food Webs
- Baltic coastal carbon cycling
